Module reference ​

HiveBoard contains 13 scored attachment configurations across three task families. The ball valve is evaluated both without and with a friction ring, producing two separate trial sets.

Torque tasks ​

IDAttachmentRequired outcomeTimeout
valve_ballBall valveRotate the handle 90° from closed to open60 s
valve_ball_ringBall valve with friction ringRotate the handle 90° with the ring fitted90 s
valve_gate_smallSmall gate valveComplete one full turn of the stem90 s
valve_gate_largeLarge gate valveComplete one full turn of the stem120 s
circuit_breakerCircuit breakerMove the toggle to the other state and hold it60 s

The gate-valve criterion is one complete stem rotation, not full travel. The number of turns required for full travel can vary with the printed thread pitch.

Precision tasks ​

IDAttachmentRequired outcomeTimeout
light_bulbLight bulb and socketThread the bulb until it is seated120 s
thread_m8M8 threaded fastenerThread the bolt along the available length120 s
thread_m30M30 threaded fastenerThread the bolt along the available length120 s
peg_insertionThreaded peg insertionThread the free peg into the empty socket until seated120 s

The peg task uses a threaded 8 mm pin. It is not a clearance-fit insertion task.

Composed assembly tasks ​

These tasks are scored by the last completed stage as well as by full success.

IDAttachmentStage 1Stage 2Stage 3Timeout
buttonCovered buttonOpen coverPress button—60 s
lockLock and keyGrasp keyInsert key verticallyRotate to unlock180 s
drawerSliding drawerGrasp handlePull openPush closed120 s
shock_absorberShock absorberGrasp pinAlign with holeInsert fully180 s

Record 0 when no stage is completed. A full success reaches the final stage listed for the attachment.

Printable files ​

The module folders and their components are available under STL/. The same high-level folder names are used under CAD/ and Simulation/ where the corresponding assets are available.

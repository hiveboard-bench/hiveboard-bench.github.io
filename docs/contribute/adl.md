# Open call: activities of daily living

We seek functional assets representing **activities of daily living (ADL)** for evaluation with robot grippers, dexterous hands, and prosthetic hands.

The asset should reproduce a useful part of an everyday task, with a repeatable initial state and an observable success condition. A working closure, for example, should require the intended release motion and retain the object when closed.

## Suitable proposals

Possible directions include:

- opening and closing container lids or household latches;
- fastening and releasing buckles or clothing fasteners;
- pressing a dispenser and collecting the released item; and
- manipulating household controls with a defined sequence of motions.

These are examples to discuss with the authors, not a list of approved designs. Explain what manipulation requirement the asset adds beyond the [existing mechanisms](/hardware/modules).

## Design requirements

- Reuse the HiveBoard mounting interface and specify the space required around the asset.
- Define the grasping surfaces, moving parts, travel limits, and operating sequence.
- State whether the task uses one hand or two. A two-handed task can also be discussed under the [bimanual call](/contribute/bimanual).
- Make the success state visible to an external camera.
- Provide a reset procedure that another laboratory can follow without adjusting the task difficulty between trials.
- Document wear, consumables, non-printed parts, and any precautions required during use.

## Propose an asset

Email [ricardo.godoy@usp.br](mailto:ricardo.godoy@usp.br?subject=HiveBoard%20ADL%20asset%20proposal) with:

1. Your name, affiliation if applicable, and proposed asset name.
2. The everyday task and the motions needed to complete it.
3. A sketch, reference image, or existing prototype.
4. The initial state, success condition, and reset procedure.
5. The files and physical or simulation testing you expect to provide.

**Contact the authors before starting detailed design** to check suitability and coordinate overlapping proposals. See [requirements and credit](/contribute/requirements) for the complete submission package and authorship eligibility.
